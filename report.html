What to Include

Helpful details

Reports are easier to review when they include clear details and context.

  • Names of people or businesses involved.
  • Listing title, request ID, conversation ID, or event name.
  • Screenshots or message details.
  • Dates, times, and location details.
  • Payment, invoice, or booking information if relevant.
  • Why you believe the situation is unsafe, fraudulent, or against policy.
