Safety Guidelines

RentEvent helps customers, vendors, and planners coordinate event services more confidently with clear expectations, safer communication, and marketplace reporting tools.

For emergencies

RentEvent is not an emergency service. If there is an immediate threat, injury, fire, crime, medical emergency, or unsafe situation, call 911 or local emergency services first.

Last updated: June 2026

1. Safety Is Shared

RentEvent provides marketplace and planning tools, but every user is responsible for making safe choices, asking questions, reviewing details, following laws, and using reasonable judgment.

Customers, vendors, and planners should confirm event details, requirements, venue rules, timing, access, insurance needs, setup limitations, payment expectations, and cancellation terms before an event.

Customers Review vendors carefully, ask questions, confirm expectations, and make sure the service is appropriate for your event.
Vendors Keep listings accurate, follow laws, maintain required licenses or insurance, and communicate clearly about safety limits.
Planners Coordinate details responsibly and verify vendor requirements, client needs, venues, and event logistics.
RentEvent Provides tools, reporting options, account controls, and marketplace review processes, but does not perform every vendor service.

2. Vendor Responsibility

Vendors are independent businesses or professionals. Vendors are responsible for their own services, staff, equipment, supplies, vehicles, insurance, permits, certifications, licenses, safety protocols, and compliance with applicable laws.

  • Use accurate business names, service descriptions, photos, and pricing.
  • Do not misrepresent availability, experience, insurance, certifications, or licenses.
  • Bring proper equipment, staffing, and supplies for the service you agreed to provide.
  • Follow venue rules, local laws, fire codes, alcohol rules, food safety rules, pool safety rules, and transportation requirements where applicable.
  • Communicate limitations clearly before accepting a booking.

3. Professional Transportation Only

RentEvent transportation categories are for professional event logistics providers, not random individual rideshare-style drivers. Transportation vendors should be appropriate businesses or providers for event transportation needs.

  • Licensed and insured shuttle companies.
  • Party bus companies.
  • Limo or chauffeur companies.
  • Valet and parking companies.
  • Venue or event transportation providers.
  • Professional wedding, corporate, festival, or guest transportation providers.

Transportation vendors should comply with applicable licensing, insurance, vehicle, driver, safety, and local legal requirements. Customers and planners should confirm details before booking.

4. Staffing, Security, and Safety-Sensitive Services

Some event services carry higher safety responsibilities. These may include lifeguards, security, off-duty police officers, valet or parking teams, bartenders, servers, setup crews, cleanup crews, drivers, shuttle providers, and other event support personnel.

  • Confirm training, certifications, licensing, insurance, and staffing levels where appropriate.
  • Confirm who is responsible for crowd control, guest access, alcohol service, water safety, parking flow, and emergency procedures.
  • Use qualified providers for pools, alcohol service, transportation, security, and large events.
  • Do not rely only on a listing description when a service involves safety-sensitive duties.

5. Booking Requests and Messaging

Use RentEvent messages and booking request tools to keep important event details documented. Good communication can prevent misunderstandings.

  • Confirm date, time, venue, service address, parking, load-in/load-out details, and setup requirements.
  • Ask about travel fees, deposits, cancellation rules, overtime charges, weather plans, and venue restrictions.
  • Keep communication professional and respectful.
  • Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the booking.
  • Report concerning, threatening, fraudulent, or inappropriate messages.

6. Payments and Off-Platform Risk

RentEvent may offer payment or billing tools as the platform develops. When available, using platform-supported payment tools may help create clearer records.

Be cautious with unusual payment requests, pressure tactics, requests to avoid documented communication, or requests that feel unsafe or suspicious. Keep records of agreements, invoices, deposits, receipts, and vendor terms.

7. Prohibited or Unsafe Activity

Users may not use RentEvent to post, request, coordinate, or promote unlawful, unsafe, misleading, abusive, discriminatory, or fraudulent activity.

  • No fake listings, fake businesses, fake reviews, or impersonation.
  • No harassment, threats, hate speech, stalking, intimidation, or abusive conduct.
  • No knowingly unsafe event services or reckless instructions.
  • No illegal substances, stolen goods, counterfeit goods, unlawful weapons, or prohibited services.
  • No misleading transportation listings or random gig-driver style ride offers.
  • No attempts to bypass platform safety tools, reports, verification, or account restrictions.
Report unsafe conduct

If you see a suspicious listing, unsafe vendor, scam attempt, harassment, or policy violation, report it through RentEvent so it can be reviewed.

8. Verifying Vendors

RentEvent may offer vendor verification tools, but verification does not guarantee that a vendor is perfect, available, insured for every scenario, legally compliant in every location, or suitable for your event.

Customers and planners should ask vendors for proof of insurance, licenses, permits, certifications, references, contracts, or other documentation when relevant.

9. Venue and Local Rules

Event venues may have rules about setup, insurance, vendors, alcohol, food, parking, music, noise, tents, fire, candles, pool use, security, cleanup, and vendor access. Users are responsible for confirming and following venue rules.

Vendors should notify customers or planners if their service requires special access, power, water, permits, clear space, staffing, weather protection, parking, or venue approval.

10. AI Planning Safety

RentEvent may provide AI planning suggestions, checklists, timelines, budgets, and vendor recommendations. AI tools are for planning support only.

Always verify vendor qualifications, event safety requirements, local laws, insurance needs, venue rules, pricing, and availability before relying on AI-generated suggestions.

11. Reporting a Concern

Report concerns if you experience or notice unsafe conduct, fraud, suspicious messages, misleading listings, harassment, policy violations, no-shows, or other marketplace issues.

RentEvent may review reports, contact users, request documentation, hide listings, restrict accounts, preserve records, or take other action when appropriate.
